Research

Current work

Quantifying global migratory connectivity of marine megafauna, and establishing methods to build migratory network models by integrating multiple data collection methods.

I co-lead Blue Corridors for Turtles with WWF and partners, combining satellite tracking and genetic data to map the movements of all seven sea turtle species and identify Important Marine Turtle Areas. Read the June 2025 report.

I’m also tracking seabirds in the Great Barrier Reef with BirdLife Australia and Queensland Parks and Wildlife Service.

I have been invited to review for journals including Animal Behaviour, Conservation Biology and MEPS.

PhD

My PhD (completed 2022) used a substantial body of existing seabird tracking data to determine the role of habitat accessibility and preference in explaining variation in distribution patterns, both within and between seabird species.

Research assistant

I conducted quality assurance and control on several seabird population distribution databases (spanning 500 individual populations and 60 years) in preparation for further analysis.

Honours

I investigated the physiological and environmental factors influencing the field body temperature and movement patterns of estuarine crocodiles in the Wenlock River system, Northern Queensland. This project involved refining and analysing over 9 million raw data points in R (including GAM, GLMM, and FFT analyses, and data visualisation using ggplot2).

Undergraduate project

I studied the frequency of skin sloughing in two native Australian amphibians, and its relationship with body size.

Volunteering

I’ve also assisted with data collection for projects on kangaroo social behaviour and endocrinology, as well as native fish passage through culverts.
